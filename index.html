About

A little more context

I'm a BSc Software Engineering student at Africa University's College of Engineering and Applied Sciences, in Mutare. Most of what I build lives on the backend — the part nobody screenshots: schemas, endpoints, the logic that decides whether something is allowed to happen.

That's shown up as a certificate-verification portal built around cryptographic hashing, a 3D reconstruction of my own campus, and a dashboard for tracking energy flow between electric vehicles and the grid. Different problems, same instinct: model the data properly first, then build on top of it.
